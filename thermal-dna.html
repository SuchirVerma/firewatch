Is this heat normal here?

Every place has a thermal signature: how hot it usually runs at this time of year, whether it burns every season, whether heat returns to the same point at night. Pick a location and FireWatch reads that signature from ten years of records and seven days of live satellite passes, then says whether what it sees now is abnormal for that place.
