Directions

    What the time is made of.

    Road distance and free-flow time from OpenStreetMap, then three printed factors: traffic for the hour, weather now, and right of way for an emergency vehicle.

    What traffic means here.

    A typical hour-of-day profile, not live congestion. For live traffic, hand off to Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze with one tap.

    What live means.

    With follow on, the page tracks your GPS, updates distance and arrival, and re-routes if you leave the line by more than 80 metres.
