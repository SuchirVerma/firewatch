FireWatchThe problem

Two fires, one pixel.

A satellite radiometer reports heat, not intent. Every winter the same thermal signature comes from a paddy field being cleared and from a factory losing containment. Only the second one is an emergency.

Field teams in Punjab visited 40,557 alert sites in one season and found no fire at 17,832 of them.

Punjab Remote Sensing Centre figures, reported by The Tribune.

The stakes

Alert fatigue is how real fires get missed.

45%

Nearly half of one season's satellite alerts in Punjab had no fire when a team arrived. Teams stop trusting the feed.

40+

Workers killed in the Sigachi Industries fire in June 2025. Industrial fires are rare, fast, and deadly.

45 min

How long a family fought the Gulzar Houz fire before anyone called for help. Early, trusted signals change outcomes.

FireWatch does not add alerts. It removes the ones that waste a team's morning.
