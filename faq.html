FAQs

Plain answers to the objections we hear most.

Is this just NASA FIRMS on a map?
No. FIRMS tells you where it is hot. FireWatch tells you whether that heat sits within 3 km of a mapped factory, whether it has come back on 3 or more days, and how sure the sensor is. That is the difference between a feed and a worklist.
Why rules instead of a trained model?
Because an officer has to defend the verdict. Every threshold is written down and every row can be checked by hand. A model that cannot explain itself does not survive its first wrong call.
What happens when the live feed arrives?
Drop one CSV beside the app. It is picked up on the next interaction, no restart, no code change. The sample set steps aside by itself.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Basemaps are keyless, fonts fall back to the system, and the app runs on a single machine. Nothing on the map can fail for want of a credential.
How fast is a detection turned into an alert?
As fast as the satellite pass. FIRMS publishes within hours of overpass, and the rule engine runs in seconds on a laptop.
Does it cover the whole world or only India?
The rules do not care where the pixel is. FIRMS covers the globe, OpenStreetMap has industrial polygons on every continent, and the dashboard map pans anywhere. The sample feed shows both India and a set of world sites.
Who is this built for?
The district industrial-safety authority and the pollution control board. The people who send the team.
